## FORM 1: ACADEMIC STAFF APER FORM

### PART 1 — Filled by Academic Staff Member

**Personal & Professional Details (Fields 1–12):**
1. Full Name (Surname First)
2. Date of Birth
3. Marital Status
4. Nationality

- Designation

- (ii) Rain Semester — Courses, Title, Units

13C. Thesis / Project Supervision:
- (a) Undergraduate — number of students
- (b) Postgraduate — number of students

13D. Previous Working Experience:
- Employer
- Designation
- Nature of Duty
- Date

**Research (Fields 14–15):**

14a. Research in Progress — brief description (text field, numbered 1, 2, 3...)
14b. Research Completed but Not Yet Published — Topics and Dates table
15. Publications — list in Harvard format:
- Books, Monographs, Theses separately
- Journal articles in full citation format

(See APER_FORM_-_SENIOR_STAFF__1_.docx in /docs/appraisal_forms/)

The Senior Non-Teaching Staff form follows a similar structure to the Junior form but is tailored to senior non-academic positions (administrative officers, accountants, senior technical staff, etc.). It uses the same personal details section (Fields 1-12) and includes:

- Similar OMC scoring table adapted for senior non-teaching roles
- Higher CUNASS grade thresholds
- Additional fields for administrative responsibilities

## IMPLEMENTATION NOTES FOR DEVELOPERS

### Academic Staff Form Component
- Part 1 is a long scrollable form with text inputs, date pickers, and tables (qualifications, experience, courses, supervision)
- Research and publications section allows dynamic add/remove rows
- Part 2 is visible to HOD/Dean only — uses radio buttons A/B/C/D/E per criterion
- Staff can see Part 2 ONLY after College Board clears it (academic staff)
- Staff counter-comment appears after Part 2 is visible

### Junior Non-Teaching Staff Form Component
- Part 1 is shorter — no research or publications section
- Part 2 uses a numeric scoring table (1-5 points per criterion, 15 criteria, max 75)
- HOD selects recommended action from a predefined list (not free text)
- Staff counter-comment is agree/disagree + free text

### Senior Non-Teaching Staff Form Component
- Similar structure to Junior but with senior-level criteria
- Scoring table may differ slightly — refer to actual DOCX file

### Common Rules Across All Forms
- All forms: Part 1 is filled by the staff member, Part 2 by HOD/HOU/Assessor
- Staff member fills and submits → HOD/HOU notified → HOD/HOU assesses and submits
- Academic staff: College Board reviews HOD assessment before staff can see it
- Non-teaching staff: Staff can view HOD assessment directly after HOD submits
- Staff can validate (agree) or dispute (disagree with counter-comment) in all forms
- Disputes go to Dean for resolution in all categories

### Database Mapping
- part1_data (JSONB in Supabase) stores all Part 1 fields as a flexible JSON object
- hod_grades (JSONB) stores the assessment table scores (A/B/C/D/E or numeric)
- hod_recommendation stores the free text recommendation
- staff_counter_comment stores the staff's dispute comment
- For Junior/Senior staff, hod_grades stores the OMC numeric scores object

### Form Validation Rules
- All required fields in Part 1 must be filled before submission
- Part 1 is locked after submission (part1_locked = true)
- HOD cannot submit Part 2 without completing all grading criteria
- Staff counter-comment is required if action = 'disputed'
